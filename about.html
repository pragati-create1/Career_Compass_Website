WHO WE ARE

Career Compass is a career guidance platform designed to help students make informed decisions about their professional future.

We provide career exploration, interactive assessments, learning resources and personalized career recommendations to help students understand their interests and abilities.

Our goal is to make career guidance accessible, informative and easier for every student.
