Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some common questions about Career Compass.

What is Career Compass?

Career Compass is a career guidance website that helps students explore career options, understand their interests and discover learning paths.

How does the Career Assessment work?

Answer the questions in the assessment page. The website uses your answers to suggest career categories that you can explore further.

Can I explore different career options?

Yes! Visit the Explore Careers page to browse different career categories and learn about available opportunities.

Is Career Compass suitable for beginners?

Yes. Students and beginners can use the website to explore career fields, understand required skills and follow suggested learning roadmaps.

Does the chatbot provide real-time AI answers?

The current chatbot is a front-end demonstration that uses predefined responses based on keywords. It does not connect to a live AI service.
